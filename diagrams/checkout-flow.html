Checkout Akisi: sepetten siparis, Kafka event zinciri, odeme onayi

core-service 8081 (Order, Payment, Inventory) ve fulfillment-service 8086 (Shipping, Notification). Kesikli ok = cevap, duz ok = cagri.

sequenceDiagram
    autonumber
    actor U as Kullanici Angular
    participant GW as api-gateway 8000
    participant OC as OrderController 8081
    participant OS as OrderService checkout
    participant CART as Cart Feign
    participant CAT as Catalog Feign
    participant ODB as order_db
    participant K as Kafka
    U->>GW: POST /api/orders/checkout ve Bearer JWT
    GW->>GW: JWT dogrula Keycloak issuer
    GW->>OC: POST /orders/checkout
    OC->>OC: PreAuthorize musteri rolu, userId jwt subject
    OC->>OS: checkout userId paymentMethod
    OS->>CART: getMyCart JWT tasinir
    CART-->>OS: sepet ve kalemler
    OS->>ODB: Order kaydet PENDING total sifir
    loop her sepet kalemi
        OS->>CAT: getProductById productId
        CAT-->>OS: fiyat isim sellerId gorselId
        OS->>ODB: OrderItem kaydet snapshot
    end
    OS->>ODB: Order total guncelle ve OrderStatusHistory PENDING
    OS->>CART: clearCart
    OS->>K: publish order-events
    OS-->>U: 200 OrderModel status PENDING
    Note over K: order-events uc tuketici farkli groupId herkes kopya alir
    par Payment listener
        K->>K: Payment SUCCESS ve PaymentTransaction payment_db
        K->>K: publish payment-events
    and Inventory listener
        K->>K: stok dus ve InventoryMovement OUT inventory_db
    and Notification listener
        K->>K: musteri satici admin bildirimleri notification_db
    end
    Note over K: payment-events uc tuketici
    par Order listener
        K->>ODB: Order status PAID ve OrderStatusHistory PAID
    and Shipping listener
        K->>K: Shipment PREPARING trackingNumber UUID shipping_db
    and Notification listener
        K->>K: Odemeniz Basarili bildirimi
    end

Sonuc: orders.status PENDING sonra PAID; payments.status SUCCESS; stok duser; shipments 1 kayit PREPARING; notifications birden cok kayit.

Not: Kullanici order-events yayinlaninca hemen 200 alir; odeme, stok ve kargo arka planda Kafka ile islenir.
